ON Partylist

Oriented Nexus Partylist

ON Partylist is a student leadership group for the Computer Society of the BSIT Department focused on connection, service, transparency, and innovation. It aims to become the central link between students, officers, and the organization.

Nexus means a central connection or link between people, ideas, and systems. For ON Partylist, it represents unity, communication, and shared leadership within the Computer Society.

Leadership Foundation

ON Partylist is inspired by Quantum Leadership Theory by Danah Zohar, which emphasizes connection, relationships, adaptability, and the power of small actions to create meaningful change.

Core Principles

Interconnectedness – Every student concern affects the whole community.

Non-Linear Impact – Small initiatives can create big improvements.

Relational Power – Strong leadership comes from strong connections.

Vision

To create a connected, service-oriented, and innovative Computer Society where every BSIT student feels heard and valued.

Mission

To strengthen student connections through quality service, proactive leadership, transparency, and innovative programs.

Advocacies

Quality Service – Organized and responsive student service.

Proactive Leadership – Taking action before problems grow.

Transparency – Keeping students informed and involved.

Innovation – Creating modern and practical solutions.

Student Connection – Building unity among BSIT students.

Possible Campaign Slogans
